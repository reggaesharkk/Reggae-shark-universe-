- **Discrete Loewner band-truncation:** a finite-dimensional source draft states a curvature-controlled trace bound with constant 12 under explicit difference-quotient matrix hypotheses. Novelty is unresolved; an arXiv submission/PDF is not represented as published.
- **Riemann/WP84:** strict greater-than-79-percent simple-zero theorem remains open. The 68.820273% route is conditional and 79.627% is a model frontier.
- **Physics:** Gate 0's observer-to-horizon bridge remains unresolved in generality. A later proof audit has not verified the identity needed to transfer the displayed positive rational expression to the first Lyapunov coefficient; coefficient positivity alone is insufficient.
- **Navier–Stokes:** finite Galerkin certificates and diagnostics do not establish arbitrary-data continuum regularity.
- **Psi:** archival protocol/software results are not evidence of artificial consciousness.
