## Exact or analytically scoped findings

1. **Deterministic C² discrete Loewner band truncation.** For a specified symmetric Loewner matrix with off-diagonal entries `(alpha_i-alpha_j)/(pi(j-i))`, its hard `R`-band truncation, `q=||alpha||²/n`, and any real `C²` test function of bounded curvature `M=||f''||∞`:
   
   `|tau f(H)-tau f(H_R)| <= 12 q M/(pi² R)`.
   
   The proof is analytic and deterministic for the given matrix class. Standard trace Taylor and commutator arguments are classical; the exact specialized discrete-Loewner combination and constant 12 are candidate original contributions, **not independently cleared for priority**. See [functional band theorem](https://github.com/reggaesharkk/riemann-bandwidth1-verification/blob/v0.1.0/notes/WP84_FUNCTIONAL_BAND_THEOREM_DELTA80.md) and [novelty audit](https://github.com/reggaesharkk/riemann-bandwidth1-verification/blob/v0.1.0/notes/WP84_PUBLICATION_READINESS_AUDIT.md).

2. **Pointwise connected five-cycle (C5) certificate.** The specified exact 150-term piecewise-affine cycle kernel satisfies `0 <= C5(v) <= max(s(v)-1,0)` on its outer support, where `s(v)=sum |v_i|/2`, so `C5=0` for `s<=1`. Exact rational enumeration covers 148,995 candidate hyperplane intersections and 151 relevant vertices. This certifies a finite kernel identity, **not** the transport of model fifth moments to actual zeta moments. [Proof and certificate](https://github.com/reggaesharkk/riemann-bandwidth1-verification/blob/v0.1.0/notes/WP84_C5_POINTWISE_SUPERCRITICAL_SIGN.md).

3. **Finite filtered-fourth connected-kernel counterexamples.** Explicit geometric fourth-cumulant and pair-kernel sign/Gram witnesses rule out certain unconditional geometric positivity shortcuts. They do **not** disprove or settle the required source-weighted prime inequality. [Audit](https://github.com/reggaesharkk/riemann-bandwidth1-verification/blob/v0.1.0/notes/WP84_R400_FILTERED_CONNECTED_KERNEL.md).

4. **Source-specific quadratic correlations and simultaneous good-section reprojection.** Bounded-band prime-side moment control, exact alias accounting, and endpoint selection have proofs under their recorded finite cutoff/normalization/mass hypotheses; techniques adapt classical Montgomery–Vaughan mean-square theory and finite-section arguments. Their novelty, usefulness and exact dependence on the source are not established as new general prime-number theorems. [Status](https://github.com/reggaesharkk/riemann-bandwidth1-verification/blob/v0.1.0/RESEARCH_STATUS.md).

5. **Nine-site Gaussian-reference sixth moment.** A closed model has an exact Wick/pinching finite-block polynomial and a rational lower certificate. Neither the model covariance nor that certificate is a proof of a positive bound for the *actual complete* arithmetic sixth moment. [Publication readiness audit](https://github.com/reggaesharkk/riemann-bandwidth1-verification/blob/v0.1.0/notes/WP84_PUBLICATION_READINESS_AUDIT.md).
