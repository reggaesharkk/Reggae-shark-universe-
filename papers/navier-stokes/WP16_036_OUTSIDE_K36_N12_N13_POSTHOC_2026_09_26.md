# WP16 outside-K36 source groups at N12 and N13 — post hoc diagnostic

**Prince Upadhyay, Independent Research · 26 September 2026**

This analysis follows the frozen N12 and N13 K36 holdout results. It does not alter the coalition, optimization, three-state definitions, or pass criteria. The existing evaluator's `reconstruct` and `k_channel_grouped` functions were used to emit every ordered source-orbit group for each of the inherited, target-only, and full-final states. For every state, the recomputed full signed sum, full absolute mass, K36 signed sum, and K36 absolute mass were required to match the archived holdout within `1e-9` before the breakdown was saved.

For full-final N13, the 766 ordered groups absent from full-final N12 contribute **1.33380** absolute mass and **−0.52699** signed sum, versus **67.96625** and **−13.06027** for the whole outside set. Thus the difference between N12 and N13 is predominantly changes in already-present groups and cancellation, rather than a newly activated large source group. This finding is limited to these two constructed finite states.

The leading groups are stable as identities but vary substantially in value. N11's outside signed residual was near zero (+0.33379), so a ratio such as “40× growth” to N13 is dominated by its small denominator. In fact, the full-final outside residual has smaller magnitude at N13 (−13.06027) than N12 (−14.74505). These two cutoffs establish no monotonic trend.

This diagnostic gives neither a bound uniform in N nor a trajectory result. A uniform statement would need control over the growing set of outside groups and signed cancellation without presupposing the high-norm estimates being sought. The 36 selected groups are not a distinguished spectral cutoff: N11's absolute-mass 90% threshold is reached by rank 24, and ranks 35–37 have smoothly varying absolute contributions.
