# Prior-art boundary and novelty discipline

VRK does **not** claim that the following are individually new:

- proof-carrying agent actions or certified execution traces;
- proof-carrying operations;
- executable/reproducible papers;
- scientific claim/evidence graphs;
- epistemic type systems for AI output;
- cryptographic provenance or hash-chained audit logs.

A September 2026 preliminary screen found active work in each of those areas.

The implementation hypothesis being tested here is compositional:

> Can authorization receipts, typed scientific claims, executable evidence certificates, adversarial verification, and dependency-aware invalidation be represented in one fail-closed kernel so an AI cannot silently promote either an action or a scientific statement beyond the authority/evidence it actually carries?

The target is therefore not “a claim graph” or “proof-carrying actions” alone. It is a common verification boundary for **agency and epistemology**, with machine-readable failure propagation.

This is a research direction, not a novelty or patent-priority assertion. Any future novelty claim requires a dedicated prior-art review.
