# WP16 Dominant-Triad Nonlinear RHS Source Gate

The phase-velocity channel audit identifies the (q)-channel as dominant in several earlier states and the (k)-channel as dominant in the strongly aligned N11 states.

This gate decomposes the nonlinear Fourier RHS of the two modes

[
q=(3,-2,1),qquad k=(6,0,3)
]

into all exact ordered convolution sources

[
r+s=q
quad	ext{or}quad
r+s=k.
]

Each source contribution to (dot a_q) or (dot a_k) is propagated through the exact product-rule formula for the tracked triad coefficient and converted into its contribution to

[
dotalpha.
]

Sources are reported both as exact signed mode pairs and grouped by ordered absolute-coordinate orbit pairs.

The purpose is to identify the external finite triad interactions responsible for ejecting the dominant high-transfer phase away from its static optimum.

Finite instantaneous diagnostic only.
