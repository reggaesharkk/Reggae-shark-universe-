# WP16 Positive-Stretching Depletion Gate

**Prince Upadhyay, Independent Research — 25 September 2026**

**Status:** prospective physical-space diagnostic.

The coefficient-weighted triad audit rejected the simple idea that the growing phase-only quotient is driven by disproportionate transfer in the newest shell. The signed H2 transfer remains dominated by the older shell-4/5 core, while the quotient grows and (b_{\rm stretch}) falls.

The next target is therefore the denominator

[
P_+=\langle(\omega\cdot S\omega)_+\rangle.
]

## Comparison

At (N=11), compare two states with the same evolved modal magnitudes and polarizations:

1. the inherited N=10 phase map embedded into the N=11 support, with newly opened phases initialized at zero;
2. the final optimized N=11 phase map.

Only Fourier phases differ.

## Diagnostics

On fixed grids (48^3,64^3,96^3), record:

- (P_+);
- signed mean stretching;
- volume fraction where (\omega\cdot S\omega>0);
- maximum and upper positive quantiles;
- fraction of (P_+) carried by the top 1%, 5%, and 10% of all grid points;
- Jaccard overlap of the positive set;
- Jaccard overlap of the top 1% and top 5% hot regions;
- depletion measured on quantile bins defined by the inherited positive field.

The last statistic distinguishes two mechanisms:

- **localized clipping:** the optimizer mainly suppresses the inherited extreme tail;
- **broad depression:** positive stretching is reduced throughout much of the inherited positive set.

## Decision value

A localized clipping mechanism would suggest looking for an explicit phase perturbation that targets a small geometric set while leaving the transfer-carrying low/mid-shell core intact.

A broad depression mechanism would instead point toward a more global phase rearrangement or cancellation principle.

## Scope boundary

This is a finite physical-space diagnostic at a fixed evolved Galerkin state. It does not establish asymptotic growth, singularity formation, or global regularity.
