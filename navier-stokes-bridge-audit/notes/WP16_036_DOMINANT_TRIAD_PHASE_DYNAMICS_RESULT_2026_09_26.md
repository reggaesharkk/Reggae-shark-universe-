## Result

At every sampled cutoff step and state, the local derivative of (|alpha|) is positive:

[
rac{d}{dt}|alpha|>0.
]

Therefore the favorable phase alignment selected by the optimizer is **not locally dynamically attracting** at these finite states.

The full-final states show the same local de-alignment direction.

The viscous contribution to (dotalpha) is zero to numerical roundoff ((sim10^{-15})). The observed phase de-alignment is therefore generated by the nonlinear term.

## Interpretation

The static finite optimizer discovers a high-transfer phase configuration, but the finite Navier–Stokes vector field does not locally lock that configuration.

This rejects the simple finite hypothesis:

[
	ext{high-transfer phase maximizer}
Rightarrow
	ext{locally attracting phase state}.
]

It does not rule out transient revisits, longer-time recurrence, or a different dynamically preferred phase relation.
