# Physics v1.4 public status clarification — 11 October 2026

This dated clarification supplements, and does not rewrite, the public portfolio's historical chronology.

An audit of the available Physics v1.4/v1.4.1 source materials found a gap in the inference previously summarized in the 30 September portfolio text. The available positivity certificate establishes positivity of the displayed rational expression `N/D`; the inspected material does not establish the preceding exact identity `Re(G21)=N/D` under the stated normal-form conventions. Consequently positivity of `N/D` alone does not prove the claimed sign of the first Lyapunov coefficient. The general Gate 0 observer-to-horizon bridge also remains unresolved.

Until the missing symbolic derivation and convention-sensitive normal-form identity are recovered and independently checked, the Hopf sign conclusion must be treated as **unverified/conditional**, not as a completed theorem. This correction concerns the stated inference; it does not claim that the underlying conclusion is false.

The mathematical record remains at [the Physics specialist repository](https://github.com/reggaesharkk/physics-v1-dynamical-sector) and its frozen archives. No OSF, Zenodo, or GitHub release record is altered by this note. No rights are changed.
